# Story 6.4: Novo recorde como número destacado

Status: done

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As an Achiever,
I want my new record to be visible,
so that I feel the milestone without cheap celebration.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a game over where the score exceeds the lane's best, **When** the stats render, **Then** the new-record figure is highlighted in the accent color — a number, not an event (D-013, UX-DR-12).
2. **And** no confetti, banner, or celebration animation fires for a new record in MVP (D-013, GDD).
3. **And** the record milestone is shown as a number even across the ceiling-tier ladder (no tier-crossing celebration in MVP).
4. **And** the record highlight respects both theme contrast (accent on surface-raised ≈ 6.2:1) and the color-blind theme's shape/text encoding (E9).

> **Scope note (CC 2026-08-23):** Epic 6 runs before Epics 3/4 on the single-lane board. The Clean-lane highlight (new-record number in accent) lands first; tier-crossing / cross-lane celebration variants are deferred to playtest/v2. This story verifies/strengthens the highlight that already ships via `GameOverOverlay.tsx:71,76` `valueRecord #E8A33D` and pins the no-celebration invariant.

**AC grouping for this story (single-lane, immediate):**
- **Highlight contract (P0, ship now):** AC 1, 4 — `isNewRecord(sessionStartBestRef.current, match.score)` → `GameOverOverlay` renders the record figure with `color: #E8A33D` (`DESIGN.md:193, token table`), on the `#fff` card (`#E8A33D` is accent on surface-raised `≈6.2:1`, `DESIGN.md:218`) and / or as the only accent in the stat list; color is not the sole carrier — value is also conveyed by position/shape/text per E9.
- **No-celebration pins (P0, guard):** AC 2, 3 — no `confetti|celebrat|lottie|reward|particleBurst|shakeMs` in `GameOverOverlay.tsx`; record is a number highlight, not a banner/event; ceiling-tier crossings (`48→6`, `96→12`…) produce no celebration in MVP (GDD Out of Scope, `DESIGN.md` Do's/Don'ts).
